# Asimi application license decision

The repository has no owner-selected root `LICENSE` file. **BLOCKED** applies to formal public source and binary distribution until the owner confirms the licensing and release decision. The Community candidate can be prepared for authorized controlled use. Bundled dependencies retain their respective terms.

| Option | Effect on Community distribution | Effect on a future commercial edition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MIT | Permits broad reuse and binary redistribution. Include the copyright and permission notice in copies or substantial portions. | Allows proprietary redistribution of covered code subject to notice requirements. [MIT license text](https://opensource.org/license/mit) |
| Apache-2.0 | Permits broad reuse and redistribution and includes an express contributor patent grant with a termination condition. Redistributors preserve applicable notices, identify modified files, and reproduce required NOTICE attributions when present. | Allows proprietary redistribution of covered code subject to its terms. Its patent terms and notice obligations remain relevant. [Apache-2.0 license text](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0) |
| GPL-3.0 | Applies copyleft to covered distributed versions. Distributing a covered executable requires providing corresponding source by an allowed method under the license. | Allows paid distribution. Proprietary distribution of the same covered work requires separate rights or a separately licensed work. [GPL-3.0 license text](https://opensource.org/license/gpl-3.0) |
| AGPL-3.0 | Applies copyleft to covered distributed versions. Section 13 adds a corresponding-source offer for users interacting remotely with a modified covered version. | Allows paid distribution and service operation subject to the license. A proprietary edition of covered code requires separate rights. [AGPL-3.0 license text](https://opensource.org/license/agpl-3.0) |

The owner should verify the rights to license the application and its contributions before choosing terms or planning dual licensing. A public release also needs the chosen license text and the applicable third-party notices. [Third-party notices](THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md)
