## Distribution

Use scripts/package-asimi.ps1 to include LICENSE, third-party notices, privacy information and guides, and generate SHA256SUMS.txt plus the separate ZIP checksum.

Historical summaries describe their original artifacts. Rebuilt binaries require fresh checksums and an accurate account of completed checks. [Release report](RELEASE-REPORT.md)
